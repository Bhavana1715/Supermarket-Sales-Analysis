# Supermarket Sales Analysis & Profitability Insights


## 📊 Project Overview
This data analysis project explores supermarket sales data to uncover profitability trends, top-performing products, and the impact of discounts on revenue. The analysis merges multiple datasets (sales, wholesale prices, and loss rates) to provide actionable business insights.

## 📂 Datasets Used
- **Sales Data**: Item codes, names, quantities sold, unit prices, and dates
- **Wholesale Prices**: Cost prices for items by date
- **Loss Rates**: Percentage of inventory lost/wasted per product
- **Discount Information**: Whether items were sold with discounts

## 🔍 Key Analyses Performed

### 1. Revenue Performance Analysis
- Identified top 10 and bottom 10 products by total revenue
- Visualized revenue distribution across products

### 2. Profitability Assessment
- Calculated profit per kg (Selling Price - Wholesale Price)
- Compared revenue vs. profit to identify high-margin products

### 3. Loss Rate Impact
- Analyzed how product loss rates affect profitability

### 4. Discount Effectiveness
- Compared profitability of discounted vs. non-discounted items
- Calculated correlation between discounts and profit margins

## 📈 Key Findings

### 🏆 Top Performers
- Clear positive correlation between revenue and profit
- High-selling items generally yield higher profits

### ⚠️ Underperformers
- Many low-revenue items clustered in low-profit zone
- Some items show negative profitability

### 💸 Discount Impact
- **27% lower profits** on discounted items vs. non-discounted
- Discounts drive volume but significantly reduce margins
- Negative correlation between discounts and profitability

## 🛠 Technologies Used
- Python 3
- Pandas (Data manipulation)
- Matplotlib & Seaborn (Visualization)
- Jupyter Notebook
